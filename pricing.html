Guarded discounts

Below the fold. Only these three. Nothing else is stacked or negotiated on this site.

The only discounts that apply

  1. Cross-Stage Bundle (20%). At least one recurring Stage 1 item and at least one recurring Stage 3 item → 20% off the combined monthly recurring fees of qualifying Stage 1 + Stage 3 items.
  2. Multi-Stage Expansion (extra 10%). The 20% bundle already applies and at least one recurring Stage 2 item is present → extra 10% of the post-20% Stage 1+3 total. Stage 2’s own fee is not part of that 10%.
  3. Annual. Pay 10 months, deliver 12, applied on the final post-discount monthly rate.

Percentage-of-ad-spend fees (Hyper-Local Paid Media) are excluded from the combined monthly recurring total that receives the 20%/10% bundle math.
