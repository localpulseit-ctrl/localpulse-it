Refunds, cancellation, and fulfillment

When a refund applies, how to cancel a subscription, and what “delivered” means. No physical returns — this is a digital / service business.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This page is the refund, cancellation, and fulfillment policy for LocalPulse IT. LocalPulse IT is a Fort Worth, Texas digital-service business founded by Michael Graves. Prices stay on the pricing page; this page does not change them.

Fulfillment — no shipping

We do not ship physical goods. There is nothing to return by mail. Fulfillment is system go-live: the purchased system is live after access is granted, the MSA is signed, and First-N is Approved or N/A. If Stage 1 applies, photos must also be requested, and received when the work needs them.

Payment received is not fulfillment. Pay is not deliver.

When work and the 7-day clock start

Delivery and the 7-day clock start only after all of the following:

  1. MSA signed
  2. First-N Approved or N/A
  3. Access granted
  4. Photos requested, and received if Stage 1 needs them

First-N is LocalPulse IT’s internal go-live review for early clients of a product. Pending First-N does not start the clock.

Refunds before work starts

If you have paid and delivery has not started — MSA still unsigned, First-N still pending, access not granted, or required Stage 1 photos not yet requested or received — unused prepaid amounts may be refunded on request. Email or call using the contacts below.

After delivery starts

Setup fees are not refundable after work starts. Recurring subscriptions are not refunded for time already delivered. A cancel stops the next period; it does not unwind the period that is already underway.

How to cancel a subscription

Email localpulseit@gmail.com or call (817) 696-9203. Say which product to stop. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep the service through that paid period. We do not require a web portal.

No physical returns

There is no product to ship back. Charge disputes about a digital system should come to us first at the same email or phone so we can check MSA, access, and First-N status.

Live-in-7-days guarantee

If the system is not working, we keep working free until it is. That is the homepage guarantee. This page does not add a cash warranty or any other promise.
