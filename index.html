The leak

Leads go cold while you are on the job.

Two quiet leaks sit in most local shops: the Google profile that does not earn the call, and the lead that arrives after hours — or while you are already on a roof, under a sink, or in a chair — and never gets a reply.

After-hours / on-the-job

A form, a missed call, a map tap. If nobody answers in the first minute, that job is still shopping. The Full Lead-to-Appointment Engine is built to reply to every lead in under 60 seconds and move qualified work onto the calendar.

Weak Google Business Profile

Incomplete categories, stale photos, silence on reviews, no posting cadence. Neighbors who keep a live profile take the pack. Stage 1 is how you get found — visibility and presence, not a redesign for its own sake.
